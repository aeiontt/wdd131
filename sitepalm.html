Site Plan: Global Destinations

Site Name

Global Destinations

Site Purpose

To provide users with information about various global destinations, filtering them by region, and allowing users to submit reviews of places they have visited.

Target Audience

Travelers, students, and geography enthusiasts looking for quick facts about major cities.

Design Palette

  • Primary Color: #1e3a8a (Deep Blue)
  • Secondary Color: #f3f4f6 (Light Gray)
  • Accent Color: #2563eb (Bright Blue)

Typography

Primary Font: Arial, sans-serif (Clean, highly readable for all screen sizes).
